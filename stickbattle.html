📖 How to Play

🎮 Controls

  • 🚶 A / D or ← → — Move left/right
  • 🦘 W / Space — Jump
  • 🎯 F or Click — Shoot
  • ⏸️ Q or Esc — Pause / Settings
  • 💬 T — Chat (online mode)
  • 📱 Touch devices: on-screen buttons appear automatically

🎮 Game Modes

  • 📚 Educational — Win a round, answer a question for a power-up
  • 🎉 Just For Fun — Pick a power-up each round
  • 👥 2-Player Local — P1 WASD+F, P2 Arrows+Enter
  • 🌐 Online — Play with a friend via room code
  • 💀 Survival — Endless waves of enemies
  • 🎯 Targets — Score run, 60 seconds
  • 👹 Boss Fight — One huge enemy, big rewards
  • 🏃 Parkour — 5 levels of platform jumping
  • 🌧️ Dodge — Avoid 60 seconds of falling rocks
  • 💪 Sumo — Push the enemy off the platform
  • ⛳ Mini Golf — 18 holes, drag to aim

💎 Currency

  • 🪙 Coins — Earned every game; spend in the Shop or open Crates
  • 💎 Gems — Rare currency from boss wins, codes, leveling up
  • ⭐ XP / Level — Win games to level up; +50×level coins per level, +1 💎 every 5 levels

⚡ Power-Ups

After a round win, pick from 3 random power-ups. Each can be stacked up to 3 times. Permanent power-ups are bought separately with gems.

🎁 Tips

  • 🪙 Try the codes screen for free rewards
  • 📚 Toggle Educational Mode in Settings to learn while you play
  • 📦 Open Crates for random cosmetics
  • 👕 Customize your stickman in the Edit Character screen
